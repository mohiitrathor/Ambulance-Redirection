INTERPRETATION
Expected Calibration Error (ECE) measures the difference between predicted confidence and observed accuracy across confidence bins. Lower values indicate better calibration.

A well-calibrated model should have predicted confidence that closely matches its observed accuracy. For example, predictions made with approximately 80% confidence should be correct roughly 80% of the time.
